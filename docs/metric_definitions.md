# Metric Definitions

> Generated by `python -m scripts.build_docs` from `src/serving/metrics.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Every business metric is defined once here and computed once in Gold. Dashboards and the business assistant read the Gold columns; they never recompute metrics.

**Decisions (agreed 2026-10-04):** line revenue uses the catalog price when a line's unit price was flagged as a mismatch; only completed orders count toward revenue; only completed refunds are subtracted, on the refund's business date.

| Metric | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **Completed order** | An order that was delivered, has a successful payment and was not cancelled. | `slv_orders.is_completed` |
| **Line revenue** | Revenue of one order line. Uses the catalog price when the line's unit price was flagged (`dq_unit_price_mismatch`), otherwise the recorded line amount. | quantity × (catalog price if flagged else unit_price) |
| **GMV** | Gross merchandise value of completed orders, before discounts. | Σ line revenue (completed orders) |
| **Discount** | Promotion discounts on completed orders. | Σ order_promotions.discount_amount (completed orders) |
| **Refunds** | Refunds with status `completed`, counted on the refund's business date. | Σ returns_refunds.amount where status = completed |
| **Net revenue** | What the business keeps. | GMV − Discount − Refunds |
| **Order value** | Value of a completed order at checkout. | order GMV − order discount |
| **Average order value (AOV)** | Average checkout value of completed orders. Refunds are not included because they are dated by refund date, not order date. | (GMV − Discount) ÷ completed orders |
| **Units** | Items sold in completed orders. | Σ quantity (completed orders) |
| **Orders placed** | Every order in Silver, whatever its outcome. | count(orders) |
| **Cancellation rate** | Share of placed orders that were cancelled. | cancelled orders ÷ orders placed |
| **On-time delivery rate** | Share of delivered orders delivered within the SLA (15 minutes from pickup). | delivered with delivery_minutes ≤ 15 ÷ delivered |
| **Units sold (inventory)** | Units leaving stock: every order line of a focus SKU except orders cancelled before dispatch. | Σ quantity (orders not pre-dispatch cancelled) |
| **Days of inventory** | How many days the closing stock lasts at the recent sales rate. Empty when there were no sales in the last 14 days (never infinite). | closing stock ÷ average daily units sold (last 14 days) |
| **Stockout day** | A day on which a focus SKU's stock reached zero at any point. | min stock during the day ≤ 0 |
| **Bulk order** | An order with any line of 8 or more units (normal lines are 1–5). | max(quantity) ≥ 8 |
| **Support / confidence / lift** | Basket association between products A and B over completed orders. | support = baskets(A∧B) ÷ baskets; confidence(A→B) = baskets(A∧B) ÷ baskets(A); lift = confidence(A→B) ÷ (baskets(B) ÷ baskets) |
