# Refund Policy

Policy ID: POL-REF · Owner: Finance and Customer Service · Applies to: all prepaid orders · Version 1.0 (Apr 2025)

This policy governs refunds in the Quick-Commerce Intelligence demo network (synthetic data).

## 1. Eligibility

A refund is issued when:

- a prepaid order is cancelled after dispatch: the full order amount is refunded;
- a delivered item is damaged, missing, of poor quality or expired: the amount of that item's line is refunded.

Cash-on-delivery orders that were not delivered are not charged, so they need no refund. About 5% of orders lead to a refund.

## 2. Timelines

- Full refunds for orders cancelled after dispatch are issued within 48 hours of the cancellation.
- Item refunds must be requested within 72 hours of delivery and are issued once approved.
- A refund is recorded with a status (pending, completed or rejected); only completed refunds reduce revenue.

## 3. Refund amount limit

A refund can never exceed the payment it refunds. A refund record with an amount above the successful payment of its order is financially impossible: it is rejected by data quality checks and sent to quarantine, not counted. One order can have at most the payment amount refunded in total.

## 4. Effect on revenue

Net revenue = gross merchandise value − promotion discounts − completed refunds. Refunds are counted on the date the refund was made, not the date of the original order. For that reason, refunds are not included in average order value.
