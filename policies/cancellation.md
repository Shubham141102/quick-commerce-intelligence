# Cancellation Policy

Policy ID: POL-CAN · Owner: Operations and Customer Service · Applies to: all orders · Version 1.0 (Apr 2025)

This policy governs order cancellations in the Quick-Commerce Intelligence demo network (synthetic data).

## 1. Before and after dispatch

A cancellation is classified by when it happens:

- **Before dispatch** (about 79% of cancellations): the order has not left the store. Stock is not consumed and no delivery is attempted.
- **After dispatch** (about 21%): the order is already with a delivery partner. The delivery is stopped and the goods return to the store.

A normal cancellation rate is about 7% of orders placed. A store or day above 10% is investigated.

## 2. Allowed reasons

Every cancellation must record one reason.

- Before dispatch: customer changed mind, item unavailable, payment issue, duplicate order.
- After dispatch: delivery delayed, customer unreachable, address issue.

Before-dispatch reasons are mostly customer-side; after-dispatch reasons are delivery-side and count against the store's delivery performance.

## 3. Customer communication

The customer is told about the cancellation in the app immediately, with the reason. When the store cancels because an item is unavailable, the app offers a substitute or a refund of that item. Prepaid orders cancelled after dispatch are refunded in full under the Refund Policy; cash-on-delivery orders are not charged.

## 4. Monitoring

Cancellations are reported weekly per store and reason, together with the share of orders placed. Item-unavailable cancellations are shared with the inventory team, because they usually point to a stockout. Delivery-side cancellations are reviewed with the delivery performance of the store.
