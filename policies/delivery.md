# Delivery Policy

Policy ID: POL-DEL · Owner: Operations · Applies to: all dark stores and delivery partners · Version 1.0 (Apr 2025)

This policy governs last-mile delivery in the Quick-Commerce Intelligence demo network (synthetic data).

## 1. Delivery promise

Every order is promised within 15 minutes of pickup by the delivery partner. This is the delivery SLA (service level agreement). Typical delivery takes about 14 minutes, so the promise leaves little room: rain, traffic and peak hours push many deliveries past 15 minutes. The promise applies to every store and every order size.

## 2. Measuring on-time delivery

On-time delivery rate = orders delivered within 15 minutes of pickup ÷ orders delivered. It is measured per store and per day. The 90th-percentile delivery time is also tracked, because the average hides the slowest deliveries. Failed deliveries and orders cancelled in transit are reported separately and are not counted as delivered.

## 3. Late and failed deliveries

- A delivery is late when it takes more than 15 minutes from pickup.
- A delivery fails when the partner cannot hand over the order (customer unreachable, address issue).
- Customers with a late delivery are informed in the app; repeated lateness at a store is reviewed by operations.
- A failed delivery is returned to the store; prepaid orders are refunded under the Refund Policy.

## 4. Delivery partners

Each store works with about 10 delivery partners. Only partners with an active status can be assigned orders. Partners are assigned in order of availability. A partner whose deliveries are repeatedly late is reviewed with the store manager.

## 5. Store performance review

Stores are reviewed weekly on: on-time rate, average delivery minutes, 90th-percentile delivery minutes, failed deliveries and in-transit cancellations. A store in the bottom three on on-time rate for two consecutive weeks must present an improvement plan, for example more partners at peak hours or a smaller delivery radius.
