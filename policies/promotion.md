# Promotion Policy

Policy ID: POL-PRO · Owner: Growth & Marketing · Applies to: all promotions · Version 1.0 (Apr 2025)

This policy governs promotions in the Quick-Commerce Intelligence demo network (synthetic data).

## 1. Promotion types and approval

Promotions are category-wide percentage discounts (for example "Dairy 15% off") that run in all stores for a fixed window. Every promotion needs approval from the marketing manager before it starts, with its category, discount, start date and end date. About 20% of orders use a promotion.

## 2. Discount limits

- Discounts are set in steps of 5%, from 5% to 30%.
- A discount above 30% is not allowed; a promotion record outside 0–100% is invalid and rejected by data quality checks.
- A promotion runs for 3 to 14 days.
- Only one promotion per category may run at a time.

## 3. Measuring uplift

Uplift compares the promoted category's average daily units (all stores) during the promotion with its average on days without any promotion: uplift % = (promotion daily units − baseline daily units) ÷ baseline × 100. Discount cost is the total discount given on completed orders that used the promotion. This is a before / after comparison, not a controlled experiment: seasonality, weather and other events also move sales.

## 4. Reviewing and stopping promotions

Every promotion is reviewed after it ends, on uplift, discount cost and orders that used it. A promotion with negative uplift, or uplift too small to cover its discount cost, should not be repeated in the same form. Deal-seeking customers respond most to promotions; premium customers respond least, so promotions are better targeted at deal-seeking segments.
